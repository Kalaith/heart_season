# Heart Season UI Redesign Plan

## Product Intent

Heart Season should feel like entering a live romance episode, not opening a student dashboard. Every screen should answer three questions immediately:

1. What is happening now?
2. What should I do next?
3. Why does it matter socially?

## Information Architecture

- `Tonight`: current episode, countdown, submission status, next recommended action
- `Plan`: guided episode planning flow with quick presets
- `Latest Scene`: story-first results, fallout, and next pressure
- `Episode Archive`: past rounds presented like dramatic recap cards
- `Cast`: scannable romance targets with vibe, preferences, and relationship read
- `Profile`: grouped identity view using emotional categories instead of raw abstract stats

## Visual Direction

- Premium casual romance game UI
- Warm glamor: cream, rose, champagne, berry, plum
- Serif only for dramatic headings and scene titles
- Sans-serif for controls, labels, body text, stats, and helper copy
- Strong hierarchy: large hero moments, clear CTAs, quieter secondary cards
- Shared world activity visible through rumors, villa feed, pairings, and social alerts

## UX Priorities

- One strong primary action per screen
- Dashboard hero must show current episode, time remaining, submission status, and recommended next step
- Planning must feel guided, card-based, and low-pressure
- Results must lead with story and then explain consequences in plain language
- Cast and Profile must be emotionally legible at a glance
- Empty states should feel alive, anticipatory, and in-world

## Implementation Passes

### Pass 1

- Rework shell, nav labels, typography, and spacing
- Redesign dashboard around a single episode hero and villa world activity
- Convert planning into a guided sequence with quick presets and a live summary
- Make results page story-first with clearer fallout cards

### Pass 2

- Upgrade cast to portrait-style cards with stronger scanning cues
- Reframe profile stats into social identity categories
- Improve archive cards to feel like episode recaps
- Add stronger onboarding hints and clearer low-skill guidance

### Pass 3

- Add richer shared-world modules such as trending pairings and villa alerts
- Add refined motion and progressive disclosure
- Replace placeholder portrait areas with real character art once assets exist
